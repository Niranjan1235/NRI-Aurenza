About the Studio

We design spaces that breathe with intention

Aurenza Studio was founded on the belief that interior design is not decoration — it is the architecture of feeling. Every room we touch is an opportunity to create an environment that elevates how you live.

Our approach combines rigorous spatial thinking with a deep sensitivity to materials, light, and the invisible rhythms of daily life.

Questions

Things people
often ask

Every project starts with conversation. Here are some of the questions we hear most from new clients.

  • How does your design process work?+
    We begin with a discovery session to understand your lifestyle, aesthetic, and aspirations. From there we develop concepts, present mood boards and material palettes, then move through detailed design, procurement, and installation.
  • How long does a full project take?+
    A standard residential project typically takes 4–8 months from initial concept to final installation, depending on scope. We'll give you a clear timeline in our proposal.
  • Do you work on projects outside Bangalore?+
    Absolutely. We've completed projects in Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and internationally. Travel and coordination costs are discussed transparently at the start.
  • What is your fee structure?+
    We charge a flat design fee based on scope, plus a percentage of the procurement total. Everything is laid out clearly before we begin — no surprises.
  • Do you handle renovations as well?+
    Yes — we manage full renovation projects, working closely with trusted contractors and craftspeople to ensure quality throughout.
