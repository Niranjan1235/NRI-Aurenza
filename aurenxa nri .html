Questions you might have — answered simply and honestly.

Where is your studio based?+
Our main studio is in Bangalore, India. We take on residential and commercial projects across India and select international locations.
What type of interiors do you design?+
We specialise in residential homes, boutique hospitality, and select commercial spaces. Every project is a unique collaboration.
Are your projects made to order?+
Yes — every design is bespoke and created specifically for your space, lifestyle, and vision. We do not use off-the-shelf templates.
How long does a full project take?+
A typical residential project takes 4–8 months from initial concept to final installation, depending on scope and complexity.
Do you work outside Bangalore?+
Absolutely. We have completed projects in Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and internationally. Travel costs are discussed transparently.
Can I visit a completed project?+
With client permission, we occasionally arrange viewings. Reach out to us directly and we will see what can be arranged.
How do I care for custom furniture?+
Each piece comes with a care guide. Natural materials are best treated with gentle, pH-neutral products and kept from prolonged direct sunlight.
Do you offer fully custom projects?+
Yes — custom projects are a core part of what we do. Get in touch with your brief and we will schedule an initial consultation.
